What SummarizeMail does

SummarizeMail reads the inbox of a mailbox you connect (Microsoft 365 / Outlook or Gmail), identifies accounts-payable email such as invoices, bills and statements, summarizes each one, forwards invoices to the Smart AP address you set, and emails you a summary of each run.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address and password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider).
  • Connected mailbox access: the mailbox address and display name, and an access token issued by Microsoft or Google. The token is stored encrypted and is used only to read and send mail for the features below.
  • Email content: when a mailbox is processed, SummarizeMail reads inbox messages received in the period you choose, including their sender, subject, date, body and attachments.
  • Processing results: for each email processed we store the sender, subject, date, a link to the message, attachment names, the category, extracted details (vendor, invoice number, amount, due date), a short summary, the suggested next step and what action was taken. We do not store full email bodies or attachment files.
  • Settings and logs: your Smart AP address, schedule settings, and activity and error logs used to run and support the service.

How we use information

  • To classify and summarize your email, forward invoices to the address you configure, and send you run summaries.
  • To run scheduled processing you turn on.
  • To keep the service secure, diagnose problems and provide support.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or use your email content to train AI models.

Service providers

We share information only with providers that operate SummarizeMail on our behalf:

  • Anthropic (Claude API): the content of each email being processed, including its attachments, is sent to Anthropic to classify and summarize it. Anthropic does not use API data to train its models.
  • Supabase: database, authentication, encrypted secret storage and server functions.
  • Vercel: hosting for the web application.
  • Microsoft and Google: the mail services you connect; their own privacy policies apply to your mailbox.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others.

Google user data

For Gmail mailboxes, SummarizeMail requests permission to read your mail (gmail.readonly) and to send mail (gmail.send), used only to provide the features described above. SummarizeMail's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is not used for advertising, is not sold, is not used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models, and is not read by people except with your permission, for security purposes, or to comply with law.

Retention and deletion

  • Mailbox access tokens are deleted automatically at the end of the access period you choose when connecting (1 to 90 days), or immediately when you disconnect the mailbox.
  • Processing results for a mailbox are deleted when you disconnect that mailbox.
  • Logs can be cleared by you on the Logs page.
  • Your account and all associated data are deleted when you ask us to delete your account.

You can also revoke SummarizeMail's access at any time from your Google account or Microsoft account.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Mailbox tokens are stored encrypted and are never sent to your browser. Access to your data is limited to your account. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.
